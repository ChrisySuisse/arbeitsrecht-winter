Employment references are among the most common issues in employment law. A purely linguistic review can nowadays usually be carried out with relatively little effort. The key issue, however, is not merely to identify problematic wording, but to ensure that justified amendments are actually accepted by the employer.

An employment reference must be truthful, complete and at the same time formulated in a favourable manner. In practice, disputes frequently arise over whether duties and responsibilities have been accurately described, performance has been appropriately recognised, or the reasons for departure and assessments of conduct have been correctly worded. In reviewing an employment reference, I consider not only its wording, but also your actual role, the history of the employment relationship and the changes that are important for your future career.

I review your employment reference, work with you to prepare an appropriate proposal for revised wording and, if requested, conduct negotiations with your employer on your behalf. In most cases, a satisfactory solution can be reached in this way. Where this is not possible, claims relating to employment references can also be enforced through court proceedings.

I also assist employers in preparing employment references that comply with legal requirements. Particularly where an employment relationship has been difficult or marked by conflict, it can be challenging to fulfil the obligation to provide a truthful reference while avoiding unnecessarily damaging wording. Careful drafting provides clarity and reduces the risk of subsequent disputes.
