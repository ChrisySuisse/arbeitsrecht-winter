There are many good reasons to get married. But there can also be good reasons to end a marriage. Once you have made that decision, I can guide you through the process from the initial decision to divorce through to the final divorce judgment.

How complex – and therefore how costly – a divorce becomes depends largely on the issues on which the spouses are still able to agree. Where the divorce and its consequences have largely been settled, the spouses can file a joint petition for divorce in Switzerland and submit an appropriate divorce agreement in a relatively straightforward procedure. Even in such cases, it can be advisable to have the agreement drafted or at least reviewed by a lawyer. Matters concerning maintenance, children, assets or occupational pensions should not merely appear reasonable today, but should be regulated in a legally sound and sustainable manner for the future.

Where fundamental disagreements remain, legal representation becomes all the more important. My approach to litigation is based on clarity and determination in pursuing the substantive issues, not on personal escalation. I consider aggressive communication or attempts to humiliate the other party neither necessary nor conducive to achieving a good outcome.

Particularly where a separation is already emotionally charged, the legal proceedings should not further intensify the personal conflict. Where an amicable solution is possible, I work towards achieving it. Where it is not, I represent your legitimate interests consistently and firmly – where necessary, through contested divorce proceedings until a court reaches its decision.
