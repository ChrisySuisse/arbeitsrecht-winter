In cases of separation and between parents who live apart, the question of appropriate maintenance is one of the most common sources of conflict. Maintenance is neither a matter of personal preference nor a voluntary or discretionary payment. Maintenance contributions are based on legal rights and obligations arising, in particular, from marriage and parenthood.

The appropriate amount of maintenance is therefore not simply a matter of free negotiation. Whether child maintenance, spousal maintenance or post-divorce maintenance is concerned, the parties’ actual financial circumstances are decisive. Income, financial needs, childcare arrangements and the ability to pay must be carefully assessed and balanced in accordance with the principles developed by the courts.

I assist you in realistically assessing maintenance claims, reviewing existing maintenance contributions and, where appropriate, having them adjusted to reflect changed circumstances. I advise and represent both persons entitled to receive maintenance and those required to pay it. For both sides, the objective is to achieve a legally sound solution that appropriately reflects the actual circumstances.
