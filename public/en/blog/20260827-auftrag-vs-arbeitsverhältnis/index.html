Independent Contractor or Employee? Distinguishing New Forms of Work

August 27, 2026 in Employment Law Employment Contract Contract Law New Work

New forms of work such as freelancing, platform work, working from home and flexible forms of collaboration are increasingly blurring the boundaries between independent contractor relationships and employment relationships. From a legal perspective, however, the distinction remains highly significant.

In my article for the Lohn & Sozialversicherungen newsletter, I explain the criteria used to distinguish an independent contractor relationship from an employment relationship and why the traditional criteria for making this distinction are increasingly being challenged by changes in the world of work.
