Exit negotiations

The termination of an employment relationship often raises issues that cannot be adequately addressed by notice of termination alone. A termination or separation agreement can be beneficial for employees at all levels, particularly where financial claims, the circumstances of departure or future career prospects need to be settled on a binding basis.

Such negotiations may cover matters including severance payments, the termination date, garden leave, outstanding holiday and overtime entitlements, bonus claims, the employment reference, and internal and external communications regarding the departure. For executives and senior professionals, additional issues often include variable compensation, participation or incentive plans, and more complex contractual arrangements.

Post-contractual non-compete clauses are particularly important. They can create considerable uncertainty, especially for executives and specialised professionals, as they may restrict future career opportunities. Whether and to what extent a non-compete clause is legally binding and enforceable, however, depends on strict statutory requirements as well as its limitations in terms of duration, geographical scope and subject matter. In separation negotiations, it may therefore be crucial to negotiate a complete waiver by the employer, an appropriate limitation of the restriction or, where appropriate, financial compensation.

I assess the legal and financial position, calculate existing claims and discuss with you which outcomes are realistically achievable and particularly important to you. On this basis, I develop a clear negotiation strategy and conduct negotiations with the employer or its legal representatives on your behalf.

I have conducted numerous termination and severance negotiations for employees in a wide range of roles, including individuals in senior positions and in international employment situations. I am also accustomed to negotiating with in-house legal departments and external law firms. The aim is to achieve a robust overall settlement that appropriately takes account of financial claims, legal risks and your future career.
