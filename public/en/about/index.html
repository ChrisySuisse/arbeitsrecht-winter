About

Employment law has been the focus of my legal career from the outset. While still a student, I worked at the Chair of Employment Law at the University of St. Gallen, with which I remain professionally affiliated to this day. I studied law at the Universities of St. Gallen, Basel, Zurich and Maastricht.

I completed my legal traineeship in the Canton of Lucerne, both at a law firm specialising in employment law and at the Lucerne Labour Court. In 2021, I was admitted to the bar in the Canton of Lucerne. I subsequently joined the employment law team of a major international law firm in Zurich before moving to a smaller Zurich firm specialising in employment law. Today, I run my own law firm in Basel.

I advise and represent both employees and employers. My practice focuses in particular on termination and severance negotiations, including for executives and senior professionals, as well as negotiations and litigation in complex employment and social security law disputes. I represent my clients in out-of-court matters, demanding negotiations and court proceedings. Geographically, my practice is centred on Basel and Northwestern Switzerland. For specialised matters, I am also regularly instructed by clients from other parts of German-speaking Switzerland.

I combine legal practice with continuous academic engagement in employment law. I regularly publish and lecture on current employment law issues. Particular areas of focus include cross-border employment relationships and the law relating to cross-border commuters, as well as specialised issues in social security law.

My firm is owner-managed and deliberately organised around personal client service. I handle every matter myself. Cases are not passed between changing points of contact but remain with me from the initial assessment through to their conclusion. This means that I know not only the case file, but also the background, interests and objectives of my clients. It creates clear responsibility and direct communication – particularly when a matter is legally complex or personally challenging.

Integrity is the standard I apply to my work as a lawyer. It means assessing a case with legal precision while also being clear about what can realistically be achieved and what cannot. A legal matter is never limited to its technical assessment. Behind every dispute are personal experiences, pressures and needs that help determine which solution will ultimately be sustainable. I take the time to understand this dimension and incorporate it into the legal strategy. This allows me to represent my clients’ interests effectively without losing sight of the person behind the case.
